# Indowud — Website Redesign Proposal

**Prepared for:** Indowud NFC
**Live preview:** https://india-0-wud.netlify.app/

---

## Why a new website

Your current site does the basics — it lists products and shares contact
details. But for a brand built on a genuinely differentiated material story
("we don't cut down trees to build"), the website is one of the strongest
tools available to:

- Build trust with architects, designers and builders who research
  materials online before they ever pick up the phone
- Stand apart from plywood and generic composite competitors who all look
  the same online
- Turn your sustainability story into a real sales asset, not just a tagline

The redesign below is built, live, and ready for review — not a mockup.

---

## What's included in the new site

- **Fully custom-built website** (not a templated theme) — faster loading,
  more secure, and built to grow with your catalogue
- **A 21-essay journal/blog** with original long-form articles, custom
  illustrations, comparison tables and data charts — positions Indowud as
  an authority on sustainable building material, not just a manufacturer
- **A distinctive visual identity** — custom textures, colour system and
  hand-drawn illustrations that reflect the "natural fibre, not wood" story,
  instead of generic stock photography
- **Smart, animated navigation** that adapts its appearance to whatever
  content is behind it — a small detail that makes the whole site feel premium
- **Live search** across the journal, and a clean, numbered content system
- **Fully responsive** — tested and verified across mobile, tablet and desktop
- **Content protection** — deters casual copying of your product specs and
  claims by competitors
- **Scalable foundation** — adding new products, pages or articles going
  forward is straightforward, with no per-page licensing or plugin costs

---

## What we'd recommend adding next (Phase 2)

These are natural next steps once the new site is live — and form the basis
of an ongoing improvement plan:

1. **Interactive product/finish configurator** — let visitors visualise
   different board finishes before they enquire
2. **Project gallery / lookbook** — real application photos, filterable by
   space type (home, office, façade, etc.)
3. **Cost-comparison calculator** — "wood vs. Indowud NFC over 10 years"
4. **Dealer/distributor locator map**
5. **Customer testimonials carousel**
6. **WhatsApp / quick-enquiry integration** — faster lead capture
7. **SEO and search-visibility improvements**
8. **Ongoing journal content** — fresh articles to keep the site active and
   discoverable on search engines

## Proposed engagement structure

**1. One-time build & launch**
Covers the design, development, content, illustration and testing work for
the website as it stands today — ready to replace your current site.

**2. Monthly support & growth retainer**
Covers ongoing peace of mind and continuous improvement:
- Hosting coordination, monitoring and security updates
- Bug fixes and small content/design changes
- Monthly performance and traffic reporting (Google Analytics is already
  wired into the site)
- Steady rollout of the Phase 2 features above, prioritised by what will
  move the needle most for enquiries and visibility

This retainer model means you're never stuck wondering "who do I call when
something needs to change" — it's already taken care of.
